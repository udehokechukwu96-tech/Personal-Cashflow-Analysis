# Personal-Cashflow-Analysis
Personal Cashflow Analysis Dashboard
## Overview
This is a Personal Cashflow Analysis Dashboard built in Microsoft Power BI to help track, analyze, and gain insights into personal financial activities for 2025 and parts of 2026. It provides a clear view of income, expenses, spending behavior, and transaction trends over the time under review, enabling better financial decision-making.

## Objectives
This is not just a dashboard built for the thrill of it. The objective of this project is to:

•	Monitor total income vs expenses

•	Identify spending patterns and trends 

•	Track top beneficiaries

•	Analyze expenses by category, and

•	Analyze expenses and channel 

## Dashboard Features
# Income Analysis

•	Displays total income (₦4.19M)

•	Tracks income trend across 1000 transactions 

# Expense Analysis
•	Total expenses (₦4.22M)

•	Displays expenses trend and fluctuations across 1089 transactions 

# Top Beneficiaries
•	Highlights top 10 recipients of funds to determine immediately the top ten beneficiaries

# Expenses by Category

•	Breaks down spending into: 

o	Others: This is the sum of all the expenses, excluding just the Data Bundle and Airtime Bundle

o	Data Bundle 

o	Airtime Bundle 

The differentiation into Data and Airtime Bundles was made to visualize the total amount spent on purchasing data and airtime bundles. Overall, the total of ₦34.71K was spent on airtime bundle, and ₦164.3K was spent on data bundle (nearly five times more than what was spent on airtime).

# Expenses by Channel

•	Distribution across: 

o	Mobile

o	USSD 

o	Web

o	POS 

•	Helps understand preferred transaction channels. Mobile accounts for the dominant expense channel, about 98.25%.

## Monthly Expense Trend
•	Tracks expenses from January to December 

•	Identifies peaks and low spending periods. You can easily spot that spending dips around March and April and spikes towards the end of the year.

## Available Balance Indicator

•	Displays account balance with a toggle visibility feature

## Business Insights Summary
•	 Expenses exceed income: This indicates a negative cashflow situation 

•	 Spending peaks observed in later months (e.g., February, October and December) 

•	Significant drop in expenses around March–April 

•	Mobile channel dominates transactions, indicating strong digital usage 

•	High concentration of transactions among top beneficiaries
